HOW THESE WERE PREPARED

Each piece is a photograph of aged paper, so it arrives carrying its own
rectangle of grey-brown and cannot simply be pasted onto a different ground.
tools/chart/cut_ornament.ps1 crops the piece and converts ink density into
ALPHA, forcing the colour to the map's ink. An optional elliptical mask fades
the crop's corners so a cartouche can be cut out of the middle of a busy sheet
without bringing a fringe of somebody else's streets with it.

Re-cut with the commands recorded in design/MAP_SYSTEM.md.
